Modern cloud and application platforms also commonly pass secrets through environment variables, but security guidance increasingly recommends dedicated secret managers for passwords, API keys, and tokens instead of plain environment-based storage. This incident sits at the intersection of those two trends: OAuth trust relationships and operational secret distribution.

## [Triple-junction solar cells hit new milestone](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01149-9) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A Nature news article published on 21 April 2026 reports a new efficiency milestone for triple-junction solar cells that stack two perovskite semiconductor layers on top of silicon. The result marks progress for a perovskite/perovskite/silicon “triple-decker” architecture that aims to capture sunlight more efficiently than today’s standard silicon cells and simpler tandem designs. Triple-junction perovskite-on-silicon cells are attractive because their theoretical efficiency ceiling is higher than that of single-junction silicon and dual-junction tandem cells. If researchers can improve efficiency while also solving stability and manufacturability challenges, this architecture could push photovoltaics toward higher power output per area and lower long-term energy costs. In a triple-junction device, each sub-cell is designed with a different bandgap so that different parts of the solar spectrum can be absorbed more effectively, which can raise total power conversion efficiency and open-circuit voltage.
